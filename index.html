        How to play

          • 1You walk on your own. Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer.
          • 2Double tap one side to dash that way. Double tap both sides to burst forward. Both cost stamina.
          • 3Swipe up to say "Excuse me": up to 4 people ahead step aside. Rivals won't, and people on phones might not hear.
          • 4Timing pays. Dash at the last moment before contact: it is free and speeds you up. Say "Excuse me" right before contact: more people move, further. Near misses refill stamina.
          • 5Red shirts are rivals. They spot your gap, cut across and brake.
          • 6In Classic, Daily and Weekly, touch anyone and the run is over. Go as far as you can.
          • 7On a computer: arrow keys or A / D steer, press twice to dash, W or Up to burst, Space or E for "Excuse me", P to pause.
          • ChainPass again within 2 seconds. Every 3 passes adds ×1, up to ×8.
          • Close / RazorPass with under 40 cm / 15 cm to spare.+25 / +50
          • SnakePass on alternating sides, 3 or more in a row.+40 each
          • SweepPass 3 people drifting one way. 5 is a Mega Sweep.+150 / +300
          • Thread the NeedleSlip between two people side by side.+150
          • Bullet TrainPass 3 people in a single burst.+120
          • JukePass someone right after a dash.+80
          • OvertakePass a speed walker.+60
          • Salmon RunPass 5 oncoming people in one chain.+150
          • Rival BurnedGet past a rival after they cut you off.+250
          • 1First to the finish wins. People are solid: a bump stops you and slows you, but the race goes on.
          • 2Slipstream: stay close behind someone walking your way. Wind builds around you, then you get a big burst of speed. After it ends you need a few seconds before the next one.
          • 3Combos make you walk faster. Running out of stamina makes you walk much slower. Your stamina is the small bar above your head.
          • 4Fast strips: painted chevrons on the floor in New York and Tokyo. Quicker ground, but narrow and in harm's way. Bots want them too, and they will try to draft you.
          • 5Airport walkway: two moving belts side by side with glass walls on the outer edges. Once you step on you are locked in, but you can move across between the belts. The teal one carries you forward, the orange one pulls you back, more gently. People stand on the right and walk on the left, so pass on the left.
          • 6Your build and gear set your top speed, acceleration and recovery. Only races use them.
          • 7Fit check: match 3 or more pieces of one style, or stack one stat, for a bonus to every stat.
