Excuse Me

Install on your phone
  • iPhoneOpen this page in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
  • AndroidOpen in Chrome, tap the menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
How to play
  • 1You walk on your own. Hold the left or right side of the screen to steer, one thumb each.
  • 2Double tap one side to dash that way. Double tap both sides at once to burst forward. Both cost stamina.
  • 3Crowds flow like real ones: lanes form, people step to their keep side and brake behind slow walkers.
  • 4Red shirts are rivals. They spot your gap, cut across and brake. The panel up top shows who's behind you and which way they're heading.
  • 5Swipe up to say "Excuse me": the person ahead steps aside. Rivals won't, and people on their phones might not hear. It recharges in 6 seconds.
  • 6Beat the clock. Weaving hard costs you a little forward speed, so bursts help you make time.
  • 7Pass people for points. Touch anyone or anything and it's over.
Combos
  • ChainPass again within 2 seconds to keep it going. Every 3 passes adds ×1, up to ×8.
  • Close / RazorPass with under 40 cm / 15 cm to spare.+25 / +50
  • SnakePass people on alternating sides, 3 or more in a row.+40 each
  • SweepPass 3 people while drifting the same way, left or right. 5 is a Mega Sweep.+150 / +300
  • Thread the NeedleSlip between two people side by side.+150
  • Bullet TrainPass 3 people in a single burst.+120
  • JukePass someone right after a dash.+80
  • SlipstreamOvertake a speed walker.+60
  • Salmon RunPass 5 oncoming people in one chain.+150
  • Rival BurnedGet past a rival after they cut you off.+250

On a computer: hold arrow keys or A / D to steer, press twice quickly to dash, W or Space to burst, E to say "Excuse me", P to pause.
