# Vision

**Excuse Me is competitive speed walking, for anyone who likes skill-based games, with single-player and multiplayer modes.**

## How the lead uses this
Judge every idea with these questions:
1. **Competitive:** does it give players something to beat, or a clearer way to measure themselves?
2. **Skill-based:** does better play earn the result, rather than luck, grinding or spending?
3. **Both modes:** does it strengthen single-player or multiplayer without weakening the other?

The Director has the final say on anything these questions do not settle.
