# Stage ideas for Alpha

**Status:** chosen by the Director, 2026-11-08. **Tokyo:** Takeshita Street, Tsukiji Outer Market, Skytree Sky Walk. **Airports:** Security Lanes, Baggage Claim, Gate Change Rush. New York is complete with the subway platform. Side routes: `side-routes.md`.

Each idea has one main challenge, so stages play differently rather than only looking different. Details get settled in each stage's plan.

## Tokyo (has Shibuya Crossing)
| Idea | Setting | What makes it play differently |
|---|---|---|
| Shinjuku Station | Rush-hour passage | Waves of commuters pour out each time a train arrives |
| Takeshita Street | Narrow shopping street | Very tight, and shoppers stop without warning |
| Akihabara | Neon electronics district | Flyer handers and crowds forming around street performers |
| Nakamise Street | Stalls leading to a temple | Slow tourists in the middle, with faster gaps along the stalls |
| Tsukiji Outer Market | Food market lanes | Delivery carts cut across, and wet floors cause slips |
| Skytree Sky Walk | Open elevated walkway | Wide and fast, with long sight lines that reward planning |

## Airports (has Terminal B)
| Idea | Setting | What makes it play differently |
|---|---|---|
| Security Lanes | Queue lanes and scanners | Choose a lane early, as lanes open and close |
| Baggage Claim | Carousels and trolleys | Bags and trolleys spill across your path |
| Duty Free | Winding shop floor | Curving route, with browsers and perfume samplers |
| Gate Change Rush | Long concourse | The whole crowd suddenly turns and surges together |
| Arrivals Hall | Barriers and greeters | People stop to hug, and signs block your view |
| Terminal Shuttle | Station and train | Timed train doors, so a good route can save a lot of time |
