## Competition identity and fair boundary

Introduce a small pure `src/sim/seeded-rules.js` module (name proposed) with movement rules `mp2`, a per-mode activation-period mapping and identity helpers. Examples: `daily:2026-11-17:mp2`, `weekly:2026-W48:mp2`; dates are examples, not release commitments. Legacy identity remains `daily:<date>`/`weekly:<week>` before activation. Local new scores become `excuseme_daily_<date>:mp2` and `excuseme_weekly_<week>:mp2`; do not copy old scores. Preserve all old storage, outfit/unlock data and `excuseme_played_daily`/`excuseme_played_weekly`. Capture `{mode, period, rules, board}` on S at start and use it through results/quit/submission. Rules must select actual movement, not merely the label. A later content release uses a new version, never reuses mp2.

Use the existing Daily/Weekly period clock (`todayKey`/`weekKey`, including its existing timezone semantics); do not silently substitute Weekly Challenge's UTC clock. Freeze activation to the first whole future Daily period and first whole future Weekly period after client availability and compatibility review. For a single global server gate, explicitly reconcile local-date periods with server time before implementation: document the timezone window and test opposite timezones. Recommend making the new competition clock UTC only in a separately explicit versioned decision, not as an incidental helper change.

Old clients can keep submitting to isolated legacy boards; they must never land on mp2. New clients show the active version clearly and never merge rankings. Retain legacy boards as historical results, not a second current competition. Version suffixes alone prevent mixed scores but do NOT enforce one try across versions: current schema uniquely identifies `(user_id, board)` and allows authenticated inserts on arbitrary board strings of length 3–40. It cannot reject stale old-client submissions by release boundary or stop a fresh install taking another board's try.

Strict rollout recommendation: before enabling mp2 online, prepare a checked-in migration with canonical period parsing and an attempt ledger unique on `(user_id, mode, period)` across legacy and versioned names, enforced atomically by an insert trigger/function. Backfill existing legacy score attempts; ledger creation and backfill must be part of reviewed rollout. Permit delayed legacy submissions only for preactivation periods; reject legacy inserts for active new periods and unknown new versions; deny mp2 before its boundary. Never add UPDATE permission for daily/weekly scores. On rollback disable current seeded online competition until a whole future boundary; do not relabel queued mp2 scores as legacy. Server receipt time cannot prove when an offline run began, so this policy deliberately retires late legacy runs for postactivation periods. Surface rejection as a retired-rules result rather than retrying forever. New clients must preserve already-used local markers even when switching versions; queued attempts remain first-score-wins.

The ledger still does not establish an account-wide attempt at run start while offline: existing enforcement is device marker plus first accepted score, and anonymous identity resets remain a limitation. Do not market stronger enforcement. If strict server rollout cannot be reviewed/applied by the authorized release owner, keep seeded online mp2 disabled and do not call the rollout complete. This planning task authorizes no migration application or live check.

## Separate stage-content assessment

1. **Strips:** `src/config/strips.js` `onStrip`/`stripHasZ` are periodic without a goal cutoff; `game.js` `movePlayer` and `src/render/slipstream.js` `drawStrips` are race-gated. A mode capability shared by simulation/rendering can enable them later. NYC curb and Shibuya center strips are intentionally hazardous; faster survival contact is fatal unlike race bumps. Validate `halfAt` widths, props/crossings and readability at and well beyond LV.goal before enabling. No crowd changes are necessary just to expose existing strips, but score physics still requires new Classic and seeded identities.
2. **Airport belts/glass:** periodic belt geometry also extends beyond goal, but movement, glass containment and scenery must be enabled together. Touch `game.js` `movePlayer`; `src/sim/crowd.js` `updPeople` airport carry/containment; `src/sim/people.js` `spawnType`/`genChunk`/`beltRiders` and raceBuild-dependent crosser/flow/spawn choices; `src/render/slipstream.js` `drawBelts`/`glassPanels`. Audit `src/sim/actions.js` `reach` and collision paths for `glassBetween`: shout, perfect-dash targets, passing and physical collisions must respect a wall consistently, including its open ends. Existing endless `crowd.js` `collide` checks circles without sameRoute or glassBetween; both exclusions must be added under the content capability, rather than assuming raceCollide applies. Clear-path may pass people but must not defeat physical railings. No invisible rails, people trapped inside new glass, unreachable pickups, or reverse-belt backward travel at exhaustion. Enabling raceBuild changes crowd generation/PRNG consumption; requires new content identity and seeded fixtures, not mp2's crowd-preservation promise.
3. **Barrier routes versus side floors:** `src/config/routes.js` `routePieces`/`zoneLayout` provide finite rope/fence/queue zones; `courseTag` versions race records only. `src/config/side-routes.js` `updateRoute`/`routeSpeed` provide finite Takeshita alley (70–260) and Subway mezzanine (80–300), not routes on the five-stage seeded pool. `src/config/route-population.js` `routePopulation` and `people.js` `genChunk` add floor populations under raceBuild. Changing the rotation to expose Subway is extra content and must preserve old period fixtures.
4. **Floor consistency:** audit `game.js` route transitions, movement, collision and pass loops; `crowd.js` player's route proxy, separation and route bounds; `actions.js` reach; `powerups.js` collection; `src/render/render.js` `render` floor filtering and `src/render/side-routes.js` floor/sign drawing. Rendering currently filters floors only for S.race, while sameRoute collision/collection exists elsewhere: simply permitting updateRoute would show pedestrians on invisible or wrong floors. Player model/proxy, hazards, cues, pickups, props and ghosts must share the same route instance. Verify entrance sweep, stair speed, rejoin, and high-speed frames crossing an entire entrance window.
5. **Beyond finite goals:** periodic strips/belts can repeat, but barrier zones and side routes currently occur only at fixed z. Do not blindly modulo LV.goal: geometry, crowd spawn chunks, route IDs, pickups, render windows and stage narrowing must agree on an occurrence index/world z. Recommend initially keeping finite route occurrences once, then returning to ordinary endless street; any repeated alley/mezzanine design needs a shared pure occurrence generator, unique instance IDs, spawn pruning, safe rejoin clearances, and independent deterministic streams. Cover crossings (`state.reset` prepopulates finite NYC signals), spills/traffic, props, event density clamped by LV.goal and late-stage `halfAt` so an endless extension does not silently remove hazards or create an unfair repeated safe lane.
