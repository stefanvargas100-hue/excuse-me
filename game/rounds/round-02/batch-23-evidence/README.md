# Batch 23 evidence

Nine PNGs are real Vite game captures through Orca's embedded browser at 390 x 844 CSS pixels, mobile mode. They are controlled poses, not playthroughs: start a Race, remove the other racers, place the player, call the main event where needed (bag items pulled to 9 m ahead and set driving; gate-change crowd pulled 40 m nearer), pause, hide the pause panel and render. All nine were looked at.

- security-quiet: pale terminal hall, "NO LIQUIDS" wall signs, a few people with bags. security-event: four lanes ahead under big OPEN / OPEN / X CLOSED / X CLOSED signs with green and red floor strips and rope posts beginning. security-finish: GATE AREA sign and a crowd.
- baggage-quiet: a dark carousel belt with coloured bags along the left, walkway to the right, arrivals screen. baggage-event: five coloured bags and trolleys crossing from the carousel side with space between them.
- gatechange-quiet: long concourse with yellow centre stripe, GATE C## boards left and DEPARTURES boards right. gatechange-event: people turned and walking toward the camera. gatechange-finish: GATE C40 ahead, crowd in front.
